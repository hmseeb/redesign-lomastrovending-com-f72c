What we do

One call covers the whole building

From the machines in your laundry room to the vents behind every dryer, Lo Mastro Vending & Service keeps apartment facilities running — on land and on the water.

Apartment Laundry Facilities

Your single source for everything the laundry room needs — quality equipment selected for the size, traffic and layout of your property.

Expert Installation

Machines set, connected and vented correctly the first time. Our expert installation is one of the reasons customers notice the difference immediately.

Laundry Equipment Service

Ongoing service that keeps machines earning instead of sitting idle — backed by the customer service we pledge to every property we work with.

Residential Dryer Vent Cleaning

Clogged residential dryer vents waste energy and create a hazard. We clear the line so every load dries the way it should.

Commercial Dryer Vent Cleaning

High-volume commercial dryers build lint fast. We service buildings and businesses across the Philadelphia area and the Florida coast.

Marine Dryer Vent Cleaning

Onboard laundry needs the same attention as a building. We handle marine dryer vent cleaning along the coast and surrounding areas.
